# Technical & developer-audience writing

Writing for developers is its own discipline: respect the reader's time, lead with the
solution, show working examples, and never pad. This section demonstrates I can adapt
my voice from "business buyer" to "engineer reading docs at 11pm trying to ship."

## A note on how I approach technical content

I'm a journalist and editor first, which means I'm good at the part most engineers
find hardest: taking something complex and making it *clear* without making it *wrong*.
I work closely with subject-matter experts (or, increasingly, AI as a research partner —
see `03-ai-content-workflow`) to get the technical detail right, then I do what I do
best: structure it so a developer can read it once and act on it.
