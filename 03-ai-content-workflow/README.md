## How AI fits into my process

I don't paste a prompt and ship the output. I use AI at specific, deliberate stages:

- **Research & synthesis** — collapsing scattered sources into a structured brief fast,
  so I spend my time on judgment and voice, not on gathering.
- **Drafting scaffolds** — generating structure and first-pass angles to react against.
  The blank page is the enemy; AI kills it.
- **Editing & QA** — using AI as a second set of eyes for consistency, tone drift, and
  the obvious-in-hindsight gaps. As a trained editor, I know what to keep and what to cut.
- **Localization checks** — pressure-testing ES vs. LATAM phrasing.

The craft — the judgment about what's true, on-brand, and worth a reader's time —
stays human. That's the part of editing that doesn't outsource.
