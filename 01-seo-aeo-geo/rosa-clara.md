# SEO copy for high-traffic category landings — Rosa Clará

**Client:** Rosa Clará (premium bridal & occasion wear)
**Format:** SEO category landing pages
**Role:** SEO copywriting, in collaboration with the in-house SEO team
**Scope:** 9 strategic category landings

---

## The challenge

Improve organic positioning for high-value commercial categories and capture
qualified, transactional traffic for searches around bridal and occasion dresses —
a fiercely competitive retail vertical.

## What I did

I wrote the on-page SEO copy for the category landings, structuring each around
search intent: matching headings, semantic coverage of the cluster, and copy that
reads naturally for a buyer while satisfying the query behind the search.

## The result

Three of the landings I worked on now rank among the **highest-traffic pages on the
entire site**:

| Landing | Monthly organic traffic |
|---|---|
| Vestidos de novia (wedding dresses) | ~21K |
| Vestidos para madrina (mother-of-the-bride) | ~20K |
| Vestidos de invitada (guest dresses) | ~12K |

Plus top positions for specific long-tail commercial keywords within the cluster
(e.g. "vestidos de novia con pedrería," "elegantes," "modernos").

## Transferable to B2B SaaS

The mechanics are identical regardless of vertical: map the search intent, build the
semantic cluster, write copy that serves both the algorithm and the human. Swap
"wedding dresses" for "rotating proxy API" and the method holds.
