## Managing a multi-client, multi-contributor pipeline (Elabs)

At Elabs I ran content across **10+ simultaneous clients** while briefing, coordinating,
and editing **15+ freelance writers**. In practice that meant:

- **Owning the calendar** — knowing what was due, for whom, and what was at risk, across
  every workstream at once.
- **Briefing contributors** — turning strategic direction into clear, actionable briefs
  so writers delivered the right thing the first time.
- **Editing for quality and consistency** — reviewing everything before publication so
  tone and standards held across a dozen different hands.
- **Keeping it moving without being chased** — the part that's hard to put on a CV but
  is the whole job: making sure nothing falls through the cracks.
