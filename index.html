AI-augmented workflow

AI is part of how I work, not a shortcut I hide.

I direct AI, I don't outsource judgment to it. It speeds up the parts that should be fast so I can spend my time on the parts that need a human: voice, accuracy, and what's actually worth a reader's time.

  • Research & synthesis into structured briefs, fast.
  • Drafting scaffolds to kill the blank page and react against.
  • Editing & QA as a second set of eyes for tone drift and gaps.
  • Localization checks across ES and LATAM phrasing.

Tools I use daily: Claude, ChatGPT, NotebookLM. I built a prompt system to keep tone consistent across contributors at Blinkist.

About me

Words are the craft. Systems are the habit.

I'm a journalist and editor by training, with an M.A. in Editing, a B.A. in Journalism, and published literary work behind me. That foundation is why I can take something complex and make it clear without making it wrong.

I've spent around eight years working with words, the last several deep in SEO (since 2019). I've moved between SEO copywriting, editorial and content operations, for consumer brands and, increasingly, for tech. Native Spanish, C1 English, and yes, I can tell Spain and LATAM Spanish apart. Based in Barcelona, fully remote, happy working async across time zones.

What ties it together: I care about the sentence and the system in equal measure.
